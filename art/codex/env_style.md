## 风格（必须遵守）
Match the attached Slay the Spire 2 vanilla references exactly in rendering style: flat 2D hand-painted game art with simplified graphic shapes, cel-shaded blocks of color with only 2-3 value steps per object, crisp dark outlines on silhouettes, very limited muted palette, dark overall value range. It must look like a 2D illustrated video game background, NOT a photograph, NOT a 3D render, NOT realistic concept art, no photographic detail, no lens effects, no volumetric realism, no bloom.
No characters, no people, no animals, no text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no emblems, no symbols on flags or banners.

## 画面洁净度（必须遵守，最重要）
Clean flat color fields. Large areas (sky, snow fields, shadows, paper) are painted as smooth flat or very softly stepped color, like the vanilla references.
No noise, no film grain, no halftone, no dithering, no stipple, no speckles, no paper/canvas grain texture, no fine repeating texture,
no moiré, no ripple, no concentric rings, no wavy banding patterns, no contour-like gradient bands, no JPEG-like artifacts, no crosshatching.
Sky gradients must be either perfectly smooth or 3-4 clearly separated flat cel bands with clean painted edges — never stair-stepped banding.

Ursus tundra at dusk (Arknights, chapter 8 "Roaring Flare"): a frozen snowy plain in a cold northern empire, a raided peasant village of wooden log cabins and fences,
burning houses with columns of black smoke, the camp of a harsh imperial army (canvas tents, palisades, plain dark-red banners without any emblem),
and on the horizon the colossal silhouette of a moving industrial fortress-city.
Mood: cold blue snow and violet dusk against warm orange fire — strong cold/warm contrast, but the image stays dark and muted.
